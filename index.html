Session types

A balanced week is mostly easy running (around 80%) with one or two harder sessions. Example paces are relative to your current 5K pace.

Couch to 5K: 9-week plan

Three sessions a week with a rest day between each. Every session starts with a 5-minute brisk walk and ends with a 5-minute walk. Repeat a week if it felt too hard.

Warm-up & running drills

Do 5–10 minutes of easy jogging, then pick 4–6 drills over 15–20 m each, walking back to recover. Finish with 3–4 strides before a fast session or race.

Strength for runners

Two short sessions a week cut injury risk and improve running economy. Single-leg work, calves and hamstrings matter most. Tap one to open it in the exercise library.
